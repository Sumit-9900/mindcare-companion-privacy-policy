Privacy Policy · MindCare Companion

Your privacy in MindCare Companion

MindCare Companion is a personal mood and wellness tracker. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what data the app collects, how it is stored, who (if anyone) it is shared with, and how you can delete it. It is written from the actual code in our app and backend — not from generic copy.

1. Summary in 60 seconds

  • MindCare Companion stores your account (email + display name), your mood entries, and your favourite-resource list on our backend so your data syncs between sessions.
  • Your password is securely protected — it is never stored or transmitted in plain text. Authentication tokens are kept on your device in secure storage.
  • We use Cloudinary to host avatar images you upload, and a transactional email provider to send verification codes.
  • The app does not contain third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the in-app profile. Deleting your account removes your user record, all mood entries, all favourites, your avatar from Cloudinary, and clears your local sign-in tokens.
  • MindCare Companion is a personal wellness tool. It is not a medical or diagnostic service.

2. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the MindCare Companion mobile application (currently distributed for Android and iOS) and the supporting backend services operated to deliver the app’s features.

It covers the data our own code collects, stores and transmits. It does not cover data you may choose to enter into third-party services linked from inside the app (for example, links inside resource articles). Those services have their own privacy policies.

3. Information we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to provide the app’s features. The categories below correspond directly to what the app and backend actually do today.

3.1 Account & sign-in information

  • Display name — the name you choose when you register.
  • Email address — used as your sign-in identifier, to send verification codes, and (if you request it) to send a password-reset email.
  • Password — stored only as a secure cryptographic hash. Plain text is never written to the database.
  • Email verification status — whether you have confirmed ownership of your email address.
  • Pending email — a temporary value stored only while a new email address is waiting to be verified during an email change.

3.2 Mood entries

When you log a mood, the following information is saved:

  • The mood you chose (for example, happy, sad, anxious, angry, calm, or confused).
  • The intensity — a value from 1 to 10.
  • One or more feeling tags from a fixed list (for example, content, stressed, peaceful).
  • One or more context tags from a fixed list (for example, work, family, sleep, relationships).
  • An optional free-text note that you can attach to the entry. This is your private journal line for that moment.
  • The date and time the mood occurred, which you can edit if you are logging a moment from earlier in the day.

Mood entries are personal. They are only visible to you, and only retrieved through your authenticated session.

3.3 Resource favourites

When you tap the heart icon on a curated article in the Resources section, we save a record saying “this user favourited this resource”. We do not store any extra metadata about the action (no timestamp beyond what is needed for ordering, no reading history).

3.4 Avatar (profile picture)

If you choose to upload a profile picture, the image is uploaded to Cloudinary. We store the image URL so we can display the picture, and we store the file identifier so we can remove it when you change or delete your picture. The image is uploaded over HTTPS and is never modified on our servers.

3.5 One-time passcodes for sign-up, email change and password reset

To verify your email address or process a password reset, we generate a six-digit one-time passcode and email it to you. We store a secure hash of the passcode (never the passcode itself), together with its expiry time and how many times it has been attempted. The passcode is automatically deleted a short time after it expires.

3.6 Local notifications (on-device only)

The app can schedule local reminders — for example, a daily check-in prompt, a periodic journal nudge, or a weekly snapshot. These reminders are stored only on your device through the operating system’s notification scheduler. No notification data is sent to our servers. If you uninstall the app, or turn off notifications in your device settings, those reminders are removed with it.

3.7 Device and technical information

The backend keeps a basic request log for every HTTP call (request line, response status code and response time), plus the remote IP address of the caller. We do not write request bodies, passwords, or authentication headers to these logs.

The mobile app does not collect device identifiers (no IMEI, Android ID, or advertising ID), precise or approximate location, contacts, microphone, accelerometer, calendar, or any other sensor data.

3.8 Camera and photos

The app can open your device camera or photo library only when you choose to change your profile picture. The chosen image is uploaded to Cloudinary as described above. No other photos, images, or videos are ever read from your device.

3.9 What we do not collect

  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDK.
  • No crash-reporting SDK.
  • No advertising network SDK.
  • No third-party cookies — this Privacy Policy page itself sets no cookies.
  • No location, contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera-roll data beyond what you choose to upload as an avatar.
  • No biometrics or health-platform data (HealthKit or Google Fit).
  • No purchases, payment information, or subscription state — the app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
  • No data broker or audience-segmentation activity.

4. How we use information

We use the information we collect only to operate the features you actually use:

  • To create and secure your account — hashing your password, verifying your email, and signing you in on this device.
  • To save and retrieve your mood entries so they appear in your journal, calendar and tracker.
  • To compute your dashboard insights and weekly insights (average intensity, most-common mood, day-by-day breakdown). These are computed from your own data and returned only to you.
  • To remember your favourite resources across devices.
  • To deliver the local reminder notifications you have opted into.
  • To deliver verification codes by email when you register, change your email, or request a password reset.
  • To keep the service secure and operational — detecting invalid sign-in attempts, preventing abuse, and debugging outages.

We do not use your data to train any AI or machine-learning model, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

5. How information is stored

  • Backend database — the application database is hosted on MongoDB Atlas, our database provider.
  • Avatar images — stored in our Cloudinary account, in the folder reserved for user profile pictures.
  • On your device — only your sign-in tokens are kept locally, in the platform secure-storage facility (iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences). No other app data is kept on the device.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any form of behavioural profiling.

6. Third-party services & sharing

MindCare Companion does not show advertising and does not sell user data. We share data with the following external processors only to deliver the features described above:

6.1 Cloudinary (image hosting)

When you upload an avatar picture, the file is transmitted to Cloudinary and stored in the folder reserved for MindCare user profile pictures. Cloudinary may process the image (encoding, optimisation) on its own infrastructure. See Cloudinary’s privacy policy.

6.2 Email delivery (transactional email provider)

Verification codes and password-reset emails are sent through a transactional email provider. The recipient address and a short message body are sent to that provider for delivery. We do not send marketing email, and we do not share your address with any other third party.

6.3 MongoDB Atlas (database hosting)

The application database is hosted on MongoDB Atlas. All application data is stored at the database level; see MongoDB’s privacy policy.

6.4 Vercel (app + website hosting)

The backend service and this Privacy Policy page are hosted on Vercel. Each request that reaches our backend passes through Vercel’s network, which receives the remote IP address and basic request metadata. See Vercel’s privacy policy.

6.5 What we do not share

  • We do not share your mood entries, journal notes, or favourites with advertisers, data brokers, AI-training pipelines, or any analytics provider.
  • We do not embed third-party analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs in the mobile app.
  • We do not allow third parties to set cookies or trackers on this Privacy Policy page.

6.6 Legal disclosures

We may disclose information only when we believe in good faith that we are required to do so by applicable law, regulation, court order, or valid legal process, or when necessary to protect the safety, rights, or property of our users or others.

7. Data retention

  • Account data — kept for as long as your account is active. Deleted when you complete the in-app Delete Account flow.
  • Mood entries, favourites, and profile fields — kept only as long as your account exists.
  • Verification codes (one-time passcodes) — removed automatically a short time after they expire.
  • Sign-in tokens — cleared from the database on logout, on password change, and on password reset.
  • Server logs — retained by our hosting provider according to its standard retention window. We do not maintain a separate long-term log store.
  • Backups — our database provider may keep database snapshots for a short rolling window as part of normal platform operation. These backups are controlled by the provider.

8. Your choices & account deletion

8.1 Inside the app

  • You can edit your display name and avatar from the profile screen.
  • You can change your password from the profile screen.
  • You can change your email address from the profile screen (re-verification required).
  • You can delete an individual mood entry from the journal screen.
  • You can un-favourite any resource at any time.
  • You can delete your entire account from the profile screen. Account deletion:
    • Removes your user account record.
    • Deletes all your mood entries.
    • Deletes all your resource favourites.
    • Deletes any pending verification records.
    • Removes your avatar from Cloudinary.
    • Clears your stored sign-in tokens from the device.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which clears your local sign-in tokens.

8.2 Rights you can exercise

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you (access request).
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to delete your data (the in-app delete button is the fastest way).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a portable copy of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in section 13. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Children’s privacy

MindCare Companion is intended for users aged 13 and over. The service is not designed for or directed to children under 13.

Because it is a personal mood and reflection journal, the service is not aimed at younger children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

For users between 13 and the age of majority in their country, we recommend that a parent or guardian review this policy with them before they create an account. We will treat their data with the same care described in the rest of this document.

If you believe an account has been created by a child under 13, please contact us at the address in section 13 and we will delete the account and any associated data.

10. Wellness disclaimer

MindCare Companion is a personal wellness, self-reflection and tracking tool. It is not a medical device, and the developer is not a healthcare provider.

  • The app does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical or mental-health condition.
  • Mood entries, insights, weekly summaries and any related content are for self-tracking and self-reflection only.
  • The app is not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, or psychiatric advice.
  • If you are experiencing a mental-health crisis, having thoughts of harming yourself or others, or feel you may be in danger, please contact your local emergency number or a qualified mental-health professional. Useful international directories include the findahelpline.com directory.
  • Always seek the advice of a qualified provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical or mental-health condition.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. These include:

  • Securely hashing passwords so the original value is never stored.
  • Encrypting all traffic between the app, the backend, and external services using HTTPS.
  • Storing your sign-in tokens on your device using the platform secure-storage facility.
  • Limiting access to your data to your own authenticated session.
  • Validating all inputs to the backend and rejecting unexpected data.
  • Hashing one-time passcodes and removing them automatically when they expire.
  • Restricting administrative actions to a separate, role-protected account.

No system is perfectly secure. We do not claim to offer any formal certification such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001. If we ever identify a material breach affecting your data, we will notify you using the email address on your account where we are legally required to do so.
